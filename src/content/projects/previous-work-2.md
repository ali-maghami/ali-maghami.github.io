## Research Overview

Conducted comprehensive PhD research at the University of Manitoba's Intelligent Digital Manufacturing Lab, developing vision-based guidance systems for precision robotic drilling in advanced aerospace composite manufacturing. Translated cutting-edge computer vision research into practical systems for high-precision industrial applications.

## Key Contributions

- **Crosshair Laser Profiler Design:** Engineered custom optical measurement system for online robot path correction and high-precision guidance
- **Vision Algorithms:** Developed target-localization methods enabling real-time process adaptation during drilling operations
- **Deep Learning for Inspection:** Created automated multi-light imaging fusion workflows with semantic segmentation for detecting microdamage and drilled-hole quality in CFRP composites
- **Data Generation & Training:** Built comprehensive automated data-generation pipelines for training inspection models
- **Publications:** Published peer-reviewed research demonstrating practical applicability to aerospace manufacturing

## Research Impact

- **Precision Achievement:** Enabled drilling accuracy required for aerospace composite assembly
- **Automation:** Reduced manual inspection time while improving defect detection
- **Knowledge Contribution:** Advanced academic understanding of vision-guided robotic systems

## Technical Stack

- **Computer Vision:** OpenCV, custom image processing, target tracking
- **Deep Learning:** PyTorch, semantic segmentation, multi-input learning
- **Robotics:** ROS, KUKA robot control, TCP/tool calibration
- **Metrology:** Laser profiling, point-cloud processing, coordinate systems
- **Manufacturing:** CFRP composites, drilling kinematics, process validation
