## Overview

Developed a multi-camera 3D vision method for precise localization and shape estimation of reflective metal surfaces in coating processes.

## Contributions

- Engineered algorithms to overcome challenges with specular reflection and lighting variation on polished metal surfaces
- Implemented advanced camera coordination and 3D reconstruction techniques
- Achieved measurement speeds compatible with production line throughput
- Built robustness for varying coating conditions and surface properties

## Contributions to Manufacturing

- Real-time feedback for process parameter adjustment
- Automated identification of coating anomalies and surface defects
- High-resolution documentation of product dimensions and quality metrics

## Technologies

Multi-camera 3D Vision, Stereo Depth Estimation, Point-cloud Processing, Python, C++, OpenCV, Open3D
