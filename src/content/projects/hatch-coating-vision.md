## Patent Overview

Invented and developed a patented multi-camera 3D vision method at Hatch Ltd. for precise localization and shape estimation of reflective metal surfaces in coating processes, creating differentiated intellectual property and enabling next-generation industrial automation solutions.

**Patent:** "Vision method and system for coating processes and systems" (WO2026039905A1, 2026)

## Technical Achievement

- **Reflective Surface Challenge:** Engineered algorithms to overcome specular reflection and lighting variation on polished metal surfaces
- **Multi-Camera Fusion:** Developed advanced camera coordination and 3D reconstruction techniques
- **Real-Time Processing:** Achieved measurement speeds compatible with production line throughput
- **Robustness:** Implemented fault tolerance for varying coating conditions and surface properties

## Industrial Applications

- **Coating Quality Control:** Enabled precise measurement of coating thickness and uniformity
- **Process Optimization:** Real-time feedback for process parameter adjustment
- **Defect Detection:** Automated identification of coating anomalies and surface defects
- **Product Traceability:** High-resolution documentation of product dimensions and quality metrics

## Competitive Advantage

This patented approach created differentiated intellectual property for industrial product applications, enabling Hatch to deliver unique solutions in the metals, mining, and manufacturing sectors where precision measurement of reflective surfaces is critical.

## Technologies

- **Vision Stack:** Multi-camera 3D vision, stereo depth estimation, structured-light analysis
- **Surface Modeling:** Point-cloud processing, mesh reconstruction, shape fitting algorithms
- **Metrology:** Sub-millimeter accuracy measurement, calibration validation
- **Software:** Python, C++, OpenCV, Open3D
- **Deployment:** Linux edge systems, real-time processing frameworks
