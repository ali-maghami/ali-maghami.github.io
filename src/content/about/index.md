## Background

I completed my PhD in Mechanical Engineering at the University of Manitoba, where I focused on vision-guided robotics and machine learning. Before that, I worked at TetraGen Robotics on perception systems for automation, and I've also contributed to research at the National Research Council Canada.

## What I Work On

My work involves computer vision, robotics, machine learning, calibration, and systems integration. I'm interested in taking research ideas and turning them into practical solutions that work in real manufacturing environments. Recent projects have included large-scale vision systems, deep learning for inspection, and robot calibration methods.

## Beyond Work

I enjoy learning new technologies, exploring how AI and robotics can improve manufacturing processes, and collaborating with teams on technical challenges. I'm always interested in connecting with others working on similar problems.
