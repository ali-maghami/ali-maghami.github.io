## Research

This work explores using machine vision to guide robotic drilling operations in aerospace composite manufacturing, enabling real-time feedback and error correction during drilling.

## Challenge

Drilling holes in composite structures requires extraordinary precision, and traditional manual methods are slow. We investigated whether automated vision guidance could improve both speed and accuracy.

## Approach

We developed camera systems and algorithms to detect tool position in real-time and provide corrective feedback to the robot during drilling operations.

## Results

The system demonstrated sub-millimeter accuracy on composite structures and was validated across multiple drilling scenarios, showing promise for automated precision manufacturing.
