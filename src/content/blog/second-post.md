## Research Article

**Author:** Maghami, A. and Khoshdarregi, M.

**Conference/Journal:** Advanced Aerospace Manufacturing

## Abstract

This research addresses the challenge of achieving sub-millimeter precision in robotic drilling operations for aerospace composite structures through real-time machine vision guidance. We present methods for target localization, online error correction, and continuous process adaptation.

## Problem Context

In aerospace manufacturing, drilling holes in composite structures (CFRP) requires extraordinary precision:
- Holes must align with fastening points within tight tolerances
- Tool deflection and structural compliance cause positioning errors
- Manual drilling is slow; automated drilling must be both fast AND precise
- Quality must be validated without damaging parts

## Our Approach

### Computer Vision System
- Developed high-speed camera systems for real-time tool positioning monitoring
- Created target detection algorithms robust to composite surface variability
- Implemented laser profiling for precise depth measurement during drilling

### Real-Time Feedback Control
- Designed algorithms that detect deviation from ideal path in real-time
- Implemented corrective commands sent to robot during drilling operation
- Validated accuracy sufficient for aerospace certification requirements

### Process Integration
- Integrated vision system into active drilling operations
- Validated on actual aerospace composite structures
- Demonstrated repeatability across multiple holes and production runs

## Key Results

- **Precision Achievement:** Sub-millimeter accuracy in hole placement on composite structures
- **Speed & Quality:** Maintained production-line throughput while exceeding manual drilling quality
- **Automation Success:** Enabled fully automated drilling processes previously requiring skilled operators
- **Scalability:** Methods applicable to various composite materials and drilling scenarios

## Research Impact

This work bridges the gap between research innovation and industrial practice, providing practical solutions for precision aerospace manufacturing automation that are both technically sound and commercially viable.
