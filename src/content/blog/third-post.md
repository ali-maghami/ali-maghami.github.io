## Peer-Reviewed Publication

**Authors:** Maghami, A., Salehi, M., and Khoshdarregi, M.

**Journal:** CIRP Journal of Manufacturing Science and Technology, 2021

## Research Summary

This research presents an automated vision-based inspection system that combines advanced multi-light imaging with deep learning to detect micro-scale defects in drilled carbon fiber-reinforced polymer (CFRP) composite structures used in aerospace applications.

## The Challenge

Aerospace manufacturers face a critical challenge: detecting microscopic defects around drilled holes in composite structures that could compromise structural integrity. Traditional manual inspection is:
- **Time-consuming:** Labor-intensive and slow for large assemblies
- **Inconsistent:** Subject to human interpretation and fatigue
- **Non-scalable:** Cannot keep pace with modern production requirements
- **Costly:** Requires expert personnel and multiple inspection passes

## Our Solution

### Multi-Light Imaging Approach
- Developed a specialized imaging system using multiple controlled light sources
- Each light angle reveals different surface and subsurface defects
- Fused multi-perspective images to create comprehensive defect visibility
- Created image datasets capturing diverse damage patterns

### Deep Learning for Defect Detection
- Built semantic segmentation models trained on multi-light imagery
- Model learns to recognize micro-damage patterns imperceptible to human inspectors
- Produces pixel-level defect classification maps for precise location identification
- Validated on hundreds of real drilling operations

### Automation & Integration
- Fully automated inspection pipeline with minimal manual intervention
- Real-time defect detection enabling immediate corrective action
- Detailed reporting and traceability for quality documentation
- Scalable architecture for deployment across production lines

## Key Results

- **Detection Accuracy:** >95% accuracy for micro-damage detection
- **Speed:** Inspects holes in seconds vs. minutes with manual methods
- **Consistency:** Objective, repeatable, data-driven decisions
- **Cost Reduction:** Enables automated quality gates in manufacturing
- **Scalability:** Ready for high-volume aerospace production

## Industry Impact

This technology enables:
- Faster, more reliable aerospace manufacturing processes
- Higher quality composite structures with verified integrity

- Multi-input deep learning architecture for multi-modal imaging
- Synthetic data generation for augmenting training datasets

This work exemplifies translating advanced computer vision and deep learning research into practical industrial solutions that deliver measurable business value while advancing manufacturing technology.
